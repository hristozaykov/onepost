Child Safety Standards

Last updated: January 30, 2026

Age Restriction

One Post is intended only for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow minors to use the app.

Prohibited Content
  • Content involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors is strictly prohibited.
  • This includes any text that depicts, promotes, or facilitates child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSAE).
Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report child safety concerns by emailing issues@onepost.ink. Reports may be submitted without interacting further with the content.

Response and Enforcement

We review all child safety reports and take appropriate action, including removing content and blocking access where necessary. Where required by law, we report relevant information to the appropriate regional or national authorities.

Emergency Situations

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Contact

Child safety inquiries can be directed to issues@onepost.ink.
