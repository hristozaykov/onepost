# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 25, 2026**

## What we collect

- **Gender, age, location**: You provide these when you start using the app.
- **Posts**: The text you write in your post.
- **Messages**: DMs you send to other users.
- **Device ID**: A random string generated on your phone. It's not linked to your identity.

## What we don't collect

- Your name
- Your email
- Your phone number
- Your photos
- Your contacts
- Your location (GPS)
- Analytics or tracking data
- Cookies

## How we store data

- Posts and pending messages are stored on AWS servers in the EU.
- Your message history is stored locally on your device.
- We don't have access to your local message history.

## How long we keep data

- Posts stay until you replace them with a new post.
- Messages are deleted from our server once delivered to the recipient.
- Your device ID and profile stay until you reset the app.

## How to delete your data

Tap the **Reset** button in the app. This deletes your post from our server and clears all local data. Your device gets a new ID.

## Who sees your data

- Your post: Anyone using the app.
- Your messages: Only the person you send them to.
- We don't sell or share data with third parties.

## Age requirement

One Post is for users 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

## Security

We use HTTPS for all data transmission. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use means you accept the changes.
