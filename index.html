One Post

A quiet alternative for social media.

One Post is a text-only social app where you can have one post live at a time. When you post something new, your previous post disappears. One post per day.

Send a response to posts that interest you. If they accept, you can start a private conversation.

No profiles. No followers. No likes. No photos. Just words.

Features:

  • Filter posts by age, gender, and location
  • Private conversations by mutual consent
  • Dark mode
  • Hide posts and conversations
  • Delete your post
  • Reset your data

Simple text conversations.
